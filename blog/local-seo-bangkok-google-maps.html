Local SEO in Bangkok: How to Get Your Business Found on Google Maps in 2026

Most customers in Bangkok find a café, clinic or tailor the same way: they search on their phone and tap the first result on the map. Here is how to make sure that result is you.

When someone in Bangkok searches for a tailor near Sukhumvit, a brunch café in Ari or a dental clinic in Silom, Google shows a map with three businesses before any regular website. That small box, often called the local pack, is where most local decisions are made. Getting into it is not luck. It is the result of a few clear, repeatable steps.

Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is the single most important local SEO asset you own. If you have not claimed it, do that first. Then fill in every field, not just the basics.

  • Primary category: choose the most specific option (for example Tailor rather than Clothing store). Add secondary categories only if they are genuinely true.
  • Name, address and phone: use exactly the same details everywhere online, including your website, Facebook page and LINE Official Account.
  • Opening hours: keep them accurate, and add special hours for public holidays such as Songkran.
  • Services and products: list them with short descriptions. These help Google understand what you offer.
  • Description: write it in plain language, in English and Thai if you serve both audiences.

Photos are not optional

Profiles with real, recent photos look trustworthy and get more clicks. Upload your storefront (so people recognise it on arrival), the interior, your team at work and your best products or dishes. Professional photography makes a visible difference here, because your profile sits right next to your competitors' in the same list.

✦

Tip. Add new photos every month. A steady stream of fresh content signals that the business is active.

Reviews: ask, then reply to every one

Reviews influence both your ranking and whether someone chooses you. The simplest system is to ask at the moment customers are happiest: when they collect an order, finish a meal or receive a finished project. A small card or LINE message with a direct review link removes friction.

Reply to every review, good or bad, in the language it was written in. A calm, helpful reply to a negative review often builds more trust than a dozen five-star ratings.

Your website still matters for local rankings

Google cross-checks your profile against your website. A fast, mobile-friendly site that clearly states what you do and where you are strengthens your local visibility. Make sure your site includes:

  • Your business name, full address and phone number on every page (usually in the footer).
  • A dedicated page for each core service, with the area you serve in the heading, for example Bespoke Suits in Bangkok.
  • An embedded Google Map on your contact page.
  • Clear click-to-call and WhatsApp or LINE buttons for mobile visitors.
  • Fast loading on mobile data, with compressed images and no heavy pop-ups.

Build consistent local citations

Citations are mentions of your business details on other websites: directories, maps, booking platforms and local listings. Consistency matters more than volume. If your old address or phone number is still listed somewhere, update it.

Serve both Thai and international searchers

Bangkok is multilingual. Many customers search in Thai, while expats and visitors search in English or other languages. If you serve both, create pages in both languages rather than relying on automatic translation. It helps people, and it helps search engines match you to the right searches.

Target the searches your customers actually make

Local searches tend to follow a few patterns: a service plus an area (suit tailor Sukhumvit), a service plus near me, or a specific need (restaurant with private room Thonglor). Write down twenty searches a customer might use to find you, then check that your profile categories, service list and website pages genuinely answer them.

  • Use neighbourhood and landmark names naturally in your page copy, such as the BTS or MRT station nearest to you.
  • Create content around the details people filter by: parking, opening late, vegetarian options, English-speaking staff, same-day service.
  • Avoid stuffing keywords into your business name on Google. It breaks the guidelines and can get a profile suspended.

Common local SEO mistakes we see in Bangkok

  • Duplicate profiles created by former staff or old agencies, splitting your reviews and confusing Google.
  • A pin in the wrong place, especially inside large malls and condominium buildings. Check it on the map yourself.
  • Different phone numbers on the profile, the website and social pages.
  • A website that is not mobile-friendly, so visitors who tap through from the map leave within seconds.
  • No photos from the last year, which makes a busy business look closed.

The businesses that win on Google Maps are rarely the biggest. They are the ones that keep their information accurate, their photos fresh and their reviews answered.

How your website and profile work together

Think of your Google Business Profile as the shop sign and your website as the shop itself. The profile wins the click; the website wins the enquiry. If your site is slow or unclear, the effort you put into ranking is wasted. Our 12-point website checklist is a good place to check yours, and our website design team builds fast, search-ready sites for Bangkok businesses.

A simple monthly local SEO routine

  1. Post one update or offer on your Google Business Profile.
  2. Upload new photos.
  3. Ask recent customers for reviews and reply to all new ones.
  4. Check that your hours and contact details are still correct.
  5. Review which search terms bring people to your profile, and update your service pages to match.

Local SEO is not a one-off project. It is a habit. Businesses that spend a little time on it every month steadily pull ahead of competitors who set up a profile once and forget about it.

Frequently asked questions

How long does local SEO take to work?

Small improvements, such as completing your profile and adding photos, can show results within weeks. Ranking consistently in the map pack for competitive searches usually takes a few months of steady work.

Do I need a website if I have a Google Business Profile?

Yes. Your profile and website support each other. The website gives Google more information about your business and gives customers somewhere to learn more and contact you.

Should I write my profile in Thai or English?

If your customers use both languages, include both. Write naturally for each audience rather than translating word for word.

Can I rank in an area where I don't have an address?

Google mainly shows businesses close to the searcher. Service-area businesses can define the areas they cover, but a physical location still has the strongest local signal. A good website with pages for the areas you serve helps you appear in regular search results.
